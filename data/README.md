The metadata may or may not be used in the list in the future. For now I'm just writing it down and yes all by these two hands, cause it gives me joy.

As of 2026-09-22:I've gone through the Media Player > Cross-platform and audited them, well auditing here just means I checked the repo, current progress. Things I've noticed are that ImPlay is in a weird spot as in it's pretty much unmaintained but had a workflow commit 7 months ago, and the build for macOS 14 was successful, there also were some commits in 2024-2025, which led me to add "stale" as an option in the status. Baka-MPlayer had some commits related to ci/cd but that was for the site and also PR for new release opened in 2023 (still open btw) but the last commit for the player was in 2023 so its status will remain unmaintained as Stax assigned it. OvoPlayer on the other hand had a release in late 2025, and the commits seem to be ongoing so its status has been updated from unmaintained to maintained, also I moved it over to Music Player. And MoonPlayer seems to be unmaintained.
